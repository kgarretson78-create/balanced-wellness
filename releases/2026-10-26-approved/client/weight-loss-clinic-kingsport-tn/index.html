Frequently Asked Questions

What is Semaglutide and how does it help with weight loss?

Semaglutide is an FDA-approved GLP-1 receptor agonist that regulates appetite hormones, slows gastric emptying, and reduces cravings at the neurological level. Clinical trials showed average weight loss of 15% of body weight over 68 weeks.

Is Tirzepatide available at your Kingsport weight loss clinic?

Yes. Tirzepatide (the active ingredient in Mounjaro and Zepbound) is available through our Kingsport clinic. Clinical trials showed average weight loss of 20%+ of body weight, making it the most effective pharmaceutical weight loss option currently available.

How long does the medical weight loss program take?

Most patients reach their goal weight in 6–12 months. We begin with a slow titration phase (weeks 1–4) to minimize side effects, then optimize your dose for maximum results. Ongoing maintenance programs are available.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

No referral is required. Call our Kingsport clinic at (423) 765-1393 or book online for a free consultation. We'll review your health history and determine the best program for you.

Are there side effects with Semaglutide?

Common side effects include nausea, constipation, and mild gastrointestinal discomfort — especially in the first few weeks. Our slow titration protocol significantly reduces these effects. Most patients find side effects diminish within 4–8 weeks.
