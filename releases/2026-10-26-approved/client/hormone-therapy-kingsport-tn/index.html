Hormone Replacement Therapy in Kingsport, Tennessee

Hormones are the master regulators of virtually every body system — energy, metabolism, mood, libido, sleep, cognitive function, and physical composition. When hormones decline with age (or due to stress, chronic illness, or other factors), the effects can be profound and wide-reaching. At Balanced Wellness Medical Spa in Kingsport TN, we offer comprehensive bioidentical hormone therapy programs for both men and women.

Bioidentical hormones are chemically identical to the hormones your body naturally produces. Unlike synthetic hormones, they interact with your hormone receptors in the same way your natural hormones do, often producing superior results with fewer side effects. Our providers use comprehensive lab panels to identify your specific hormonal imbalances and design a precise, individualized protocol.

Whether you're experiencing symptoms of menopause, andropause (male hormone decline), thyroid dysfunction, or adrenal fatigue, our Kingsport clinic offers evidence-based hormone therapy to help you reclaim your vitality and quality of life. Am I a Good Candidate?

Good Candidates Include

  • Women experiencing perimenopause or menopause symptoms
  • Men with low testosterone (andropause) symptoms
  • Adults with unexplained fatigue, weight gain, or mood changes
  • Patients with diagnosed hormonal imbalances via lab testing
  • Those seeking preventive anti-aging hormone optimization
  • Individuals with thyroid disorders or adrenal dysfunction

May Not Be Suitable If

  • Active hormone-sensitive cancers (breast, prostate, uterine)
  • Uncontrolled blood clots or thromboembolic conditions
  • Pregnancy (natural hormone changes are expected)
  • Patients not willing to commit to ongoing lab monitoring

Are bioidentical hormones safer than synthetic hormones?

Bioidentical hormones are chemically identical to your body's own hormones and are generally associated with a more favorable side effect profile than older synthetic options. All hormone therapy requires proper medical evaluation, dosing, and monitoring — our Kingsport providers handle this throughout your protocol.

How do I know if I need hormone therapy in Kingsport, TN?

Common signs include persistent fatigue, unexplained weight gain (especially around the midsection), low libido, mood changes, anxiety, brain fog, hot flashes, night sweats, poor sleep, loss of muscle mass, or reduced exercise tolerance. The only way to confirm a hormonal imbalance is through comprehensive lab testing, which we offer at our Kingsport and Jonesborough clinics.

How long do I need to be on hormone therapy?

This is highly individual. Some patients find short-term therapy alleviates their symptoms during perimenopause or a stressful season; others choose to continue long-term for sustained health and quality-of-life benefits. We review your goals annually and adjust recommendations over time.

What hormones do you test for at Balanced Wellness?

Our comprehensive panel includes estradiol, progesterone, total and free testosterone, DHEA-S, cortisol, thyroid (TSH, free T3, free T4), IGF-1, and metabolic markers including fasting insulin, fasting glucose, A1c, and a lipid panel. Additional markers can be ordered based on your symptoms.

Can men get testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) in Kingsport?

Yes. We treat men with documented low testosterone (andropause) using injection, cream, or pellet delivery, with ongoing monitoring of hormone levels, hematocrit, PSA, and other safety markers. Symptoms like fatigue, low libido, depressive mood, brain fog, weight gain, and loss of strength often improve within 4–8 weeks.

Is hormone therapy safe?

When prescribed and monitored properly, hormone therapy is well-tolerated for most healthy adults and may have meaningful long-term benefits for bone density, cardiovascular health, cognitive function, and quality of life. Hormone-sensitive cancers, uncontrolled clotting disorders, and pregnancy are contraindications. We screen for these at your consultation.
