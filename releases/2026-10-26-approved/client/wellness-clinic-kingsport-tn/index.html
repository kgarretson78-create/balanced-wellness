Frequently Asked Questions

What is a wellness clinic and how is it different from a regular doctor?

A wellness clinic like Balanced Wellness focuses on proactive, preventive, and functional medicine — optimizing your health rather than just treating disease. We spend more time with each patient, run more comprehensive labs, and address hormonal, metabolic, and lifestyle factors that traditional practices often overlook.

What is IV therapy and what are the benefits?

IV nutrient therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and antioxidants directly into the bloodstream for 100% absorption — far superior to oral supplements. Benefits include increased energy, immune support, faster recovery, hydration, anti-aging, and targeted therapeutic effects depending on the formulation.
